HOW TO FLY

Throttle with Shift / Ctrl. Pitch with ↑ / ↓ or W / S, roll with ← / → or A / D — a banked turn steers the plane. Q / E add rudder. Space boosts and drinks fuel. Click the game to lock the mouse for fine aim. Below 50 knots she stalls: nose drops, controls go soft — add throttle and level out. Rings pay 100 points, 10 seconds and fuel. Land on the runway, slow and lined up, to bank the landing bonus. Fuel empty means gliding home — or swimming.
